About Us

Our Mission

Preserving the Legacy of Japanese Automotive Excellence

JDM Culture was founded by a group of enthusiasts in Chisinau, Moldova who fell in love with Japanese sports cars through games, anime, and import meets. What started as a local car club became a global platform celebrating the engineering art of Japan.
